# Gateway-Authorized D3D12 Copy Elision Evidence v0.20.0

Recorded on 2026-08-11 with FluidRuntime 0.20.0, FluidLink 0.3.0, and
FluidGateway 0.67.0.

## Scope

This release adds the first bounded native D3D12 action. A separate hook DLL
patches one owned COPY command list and may omit exact redundant full-buffer
`CopyBufferRegion` calls to one registered 4 MiB DEFAULT buffer. FluidGateway
must authorize action bit 16 and a budget from 1 through 128 before the native
final gate can act.

The controlled workload uses two distinct 4 MiB patterns in one 8 MiB UPLOAD
resource. It proves a required A copy, an A-to-B transition, an unmodeled
partial destination write, an explicit invalidation, required recovery copies,
128 redundant candidates, a final transition to `COPY_SOURCE`, readback, and a
completed fence.

## Native Safety Contract

- one owned COPY command list, immutable upload range, and copy-only DEFAULT
  destination;
- one 8 MiB CPU-cacheable shadow captured at registration, bounded to two
  4 MiB source ranges;
- the matching UPLOAD resource is unmapped with its full written range before
  command recording and remains unmapped through the completion fence;
- every candidate receives a full 4 MiB byte comparison against retained
  destination content before an atomic policy reservation;
- partial or otherwise ineligible writes to the destination invalidate retained
  content automatically; an explicit invalidation API covers owner-declared
  writes outside the modeled path;
- `Close`, `Reset`, `CopyResource`, and `CopyTextureRegion` also invalidate
  relevant state;
- policy lifetime is at most four seconds and the action budget is at most 128;
- detach restores all five command-list vtable entries atomically and waits for
  active calls before releasing resources.

The CPU shadow is a cooperative contract. It is not inferred from an external
process, and the registered bytes must match the upload range. Reading a
write-combined discrete-GPU UPLOAD mapping on every candidate was tested and
rejected during development because it made 128 comparisons take about 7.5
seconds. The final design copies the owner-provided shadow once and compares in
cacheable CPU memory.

## RX 580 Result

Adapter: AMD Radeon RX 580 2048SP, vendor `0x1002`, device `0x6fdf`, LUID
`000000000000ea24`, non-UMA, resource heap tier 2.

| Paired delta | p50 | p95 | p99 | Optimized wins |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Managed end-to-end | -23,551.5 us | -10,988.65 us | -7,843.33 us | 10/10 |
| Submit to fence | -48,442 us | -46,138.85 us | -44,852.57 us | 10/10 |
| GPU timestamp | -49,349.5 us | -47,063.7 us | -45,808.74 us | 10/10 |
| CPU command recording | -332 us | +6,363.3 us | +9,000.66 us | 5/10 |

The managed end-to-end window includes live Gateway authorization, process
startup, source-shadow registration, native policy publication, command
recording, fence completion, IPC collection, validation, and rollback. Its
baseline p50 was 428,038 us and optimized p50 was 404,259.5 us.

CPU command-recording tails alone were mixed because both paths perform every
exact comparison. The scoped performance gate therefore requires negative
managed end-to-end, submit-to-fence, and GPU p50/p95/p99 deltas with at least
80% wins. All three gated intervals passed; the CPU interval remains published
as a visible cost rather than being presented as a gain.

WARP also produced 10/10 end-to-end wins and negative p50/p95/p99 deltas. Its
claim remains blocked by `software-adapter-not-hardware`.

## Fail-Closed Controls

The same command launches a fresh all-forwarded target when authorization fails
before native policy publication.

| WARP fault | Authorization | Complete fallback | RTTs | Native result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Invalid response | 68,408 us | 693,030 us | 0 | 132 forwarded, 0 skipped |
| Accepted stall | 510,829 us | 1,135,550 us | 0 | 132 forwarded, 0 skipped |
| Cumulatively slow peer | 505,412 us | 1,133,195 us | 2 | 132 forwarded, 0 skipped |

All fallbacks retained exact content, fence completion, one automatic and one
explicit invalidation, zero policy fields, and restored vtable pointers. The
same three controls also passed on the RX 580.

## Evidence Boundary

This is a positive result for one deterministic owned D3D12 COPY workload. The
536,870,912-byte value counts omitted API-level copy commands; it does not
measure PCIe traffic or physical RAM-to-VRAM movement. This release does not
cover textures, placed or aliased resources, multiple command lists or queues,
residency control, presentation, external-process injection, anti-cheat targets,
FPS, input latency, power, Vulkan, or a general scheduler.
