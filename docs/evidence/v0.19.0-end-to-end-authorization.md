# End-to-End Authorization Evidence v0.19.0

Recorded on 2026-08-11 between FluidRuntime 0.19.0, FluidLink 0.3.0,
and FluidGateway 0.67.0.

## Scope

This release puts Gateway authorization, owned-process startup, native policy
publication, D3D11 execution, report validation, and fail-closed fallback in one
managed timing window. It adds p50, p95, and p99 distributions without changing
the FluidLink wire contracts, native hook ABI, one-resource cache, or
128-action ceiling.

The positive result applies only to the owned full-buffer D3D11
`UpdateSubresource` workload. Each optimized run receives 128 explicit
deduplicate decisions and may skip 128 exact 4 MiB repeats after the native
generation and `memcmp` gates pass.

## Complete Timing Window

Baseline and optimized processes alternate inside each pair. Warmups are
excluded. Baseline timing contains process startup, native workload, IPC
collection, and validation. Optimized timing contains all of those stages plus
live FluidGateway authorization and native policy publication. Every optimized
sample is rejected if its complete time is shorter than its own recorded
authorization time.

Adapter: AMD Radeon RX 580 2048SP, vendor `0x1002`, device `0x6fdf`, LUID
`000000000000ea24`.

| RX 580 managed elapsed | Baseline | Optimized | Paired delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p50 | 731,213.500 us | 353,670.500 us | -376,362.500 us |
| p95 | 768,342.950 us | 368,982.800 us | -356,111.700 us |
| p99 | 783,178.190 us | 372,905.360 us | -352,855.140 us |

Optimized was lower in 10/10 measured pairs. The performance gate requires
negative p50, p95, and p99 paired deltas, at least 80% optimized wins, a passing
native CPU/GPU gate, and reliable concurrent authorization. All conditions
passed on this owned RX 580 trace.

The native intervals inside the same runs remained consistent:

| Native metric | Baseline p50 | Optimized p50 | Delta p50 | Delta p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CPU workload | 511,105.900 us | 108,724.250 us | -402,522.400 us | -394,776.930 us |
| GPU timestamp | 467,191.680 us | 3,675.520 us | -463,516.160 us | -455,666.668 us |

CPU and GPU favored optimized in 10/10 pairs. Each optimized run skipped
536,870,912 logical API bytes. This count is not measured physical RAM-to-VRAM
or PCIe traffic.

WARP also produced negative complete-window deltas: p50 `-57,121.000 us`, p95
`-50,934.950 us`, and p99 `-50,095.790 us`, with 10/10 optimized wins. Its
performance claim remains blocked by `software-adapter-not-hardware`.

## Concurrent Authorization

The benchmark runs one warmup wave and 32 measured requests at each level. Each
request performs the complete ten-round-trip authorization, validates all 128
decisions, and records unique context and stable OS-bound peer identity.

| Concurrency | Failures | Throughput | p50 | p95 | p99 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0 | 42.963 requests/s | 23,189.500 us | 24,166.100 us | 24,588.720 us |
| 2 | 0 | 46.592 requests/s | 42,646.500 us | 44,833.850 us | 45,449.190 us |
| 4 | 0 | 44.322 requests/s | 87,954.000 us | 108,196.100 us | 109,870.700 us |
| 8 | 0 | 43.263 requests/s | 185,546.500 us | 213,315.200 us | 215,338.490 us |

The declared session-level p99 budget is 250,000 us. The final x8 result passed,
so the report records
`retain-loopback-tcp-for-current-session-level-control`. This does not establish
TCP as a per-frame hot-path transport. A shared-memory prototype becomes
justified only if a defined lower-latency workload exceeds its budget after
correctness and peer stability pass.

During development, immediate connection turnover at concurrency 8 exposed a
real bounded-server race: the next client could arrive after receiving the
`GOODBYE` acknowledgement but before the previous handler returned its slot.
FluidGateway 0.67.0 releases that slot before writing the final acknowledgement
and makes cleanup release idempotent. Saturation tests still reject a genuinely
excess active session. Three repeated cross-process x8 runs then completed with
zero failures and p99 values from 213,929.370 to 228,425.630 us.

## Fail-Closed Timing

Fallback reports now record authorization and the verified baseline execution
inside one outer elapsed value.

| Fault | Authorization | Complete fallback | RTTs | Native result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Invalid response | 69,689 us | 414,869 us | 0 | 134 forwarded, 0 skipped |
| Accepted stall | 508,111 us | 852,150 us | 0 | 134 forwarded, 0 skipped |
| Cumulatively slow peer | 510,221 us | 856,969 us | 2 | 134 forwarded, 0 skipped |

No failing authorization published a native policy. Exact content and rollback
passed in every fallback.

## Evidence Boundary

This release demonstrates one end-to-end gain in a deterministic owned D3D11
lab. It does not demonstrate higher game FPS, lower input latency, lower power,
physical RAM/VRAM placement, reduced PCIe traffic, external-game compatibility,
D3D12/Vulkan actuation, anti-cheat compatibility, or a general scheduler.
Loopback PID/executable binding is verified through Windows but is explicitly
not cryptographic peer authentication.
